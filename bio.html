biography

Hello, I’m Sanyat and I’m an aspiring software developer from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I moved to Australia for university, and I fell in love with the country. I hope to work in renowned company here after my graduation. I grew up in the main cantonment of Dhaka as my dad is in the army. Growing up, I’ve always loved computers and video games were a big part of my childhood. As the brain of a child is really curious, it got me a really young version of myself thinking about how computers really work. One of the main reasons why I developed a fondness for coding. I am 21 years old; I finished my high school and college graduation in 2019 and applied for Information Technology at Federation University. I am currently working at IBM as a GTS Intern and it has been an amazing experience and I am really grateful for the opportunity.
